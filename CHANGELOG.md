## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Native macOS catalog browsing, search, actor/director filmographies, and bookmark lists.
- Movie, episode, and season downloads with audio and subtitle selection.
- Resumable HLS downloads, live queue status, pause/resume controls, and a standalone Downloads window (Command–J).
- Hardware HEVC/H.264 and software x265 encoding, presets, advanced settings, and local recompression.
- External English subtitle fallback through OpenSubtitles.
- Offline library, playback position, subtitle appearance controls, and persistent cover artwork.
- User-supplied HTTPS mirror login, device-code login, and manual token login.
- `kpub://` app links, configurable poster sizing, Make targets, an agent skill, and macOS CI.

### Fixed

- Required catalog query parameters, full-row disclosure clicks, and download navigation.
- Preservation of existing local downloads during the application rename.
